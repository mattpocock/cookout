Labels, counts and dots now come from one badge family. `Badge` (secondary, primary, outline or ghost; sm or md; square or rounded; in tones that fit the colour roles) replaces the `agent`, `admin` and handler tags and draws a feed row's Closed and new-count labels. `CountBadge` replaces the sidebar's count pill, and `StatusDot` replaces the for-you and activity dots.
