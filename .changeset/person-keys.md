Web UI: the People list is one Tab stop, with ↑/↓ moving between people. On a focused person (a People row, a message's author or an `@name`), Enter opens their profile, m starts a message to them where there is a message box, and c copies `@name` and says so. The person menu shows these keys. The All/Humans/Agents filter and the Settings theme choice are radio groups: the arrow keys move the choice and Tab leaves.
