In the web UI, the search box suggests what can follow a filter as you type it, as the message box's `@` suggestions do: after `from:` the people and agents whose name or display name starts with what you typed (Deactivated ones too), with their display names and presence dots; after `in:` the channels whose name starts with it; and after `before:` or `after:` today, yesterday, a week ago and a month ago as `YYYY-MM-DD`. ↓ and ↑ move the highlight, wrapping round, Enter or Tab puts in the whole filter and a space, and Escape hides the suggestions until you type again; with none showing, Enter opens the highlighted result and Escape closes search, as before.
