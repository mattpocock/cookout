In the web UI, an identity's avatar shows wherever its initials showed: beside its messages, in the people list, on its profile, on its identity card, in thread summaries, inbox rows and the sidebar, and now in mention suggestions too. Its alt text is the identity's display name. An identity with no avatar, or one whose picture fails to load, shows its initials as before. A new avatar shows everywhere at once, without a reload.
