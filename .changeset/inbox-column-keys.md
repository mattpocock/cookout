In the web app, the column keys work in the Inbox: ← on an Inbox row moves focus to the sidebar, on the Inbox's link; → on the Inbox's link moves into its rows, on the selected or newest one (opening the Inbox if it isn't open); and Ctrl+] and Ctrl+[ stop on the rows between the sidebar and the conversation. ↑ in a message box now goes to the newest message whenever the caret is at the box's first character, keeping what you typed, not only when the box is empty.
